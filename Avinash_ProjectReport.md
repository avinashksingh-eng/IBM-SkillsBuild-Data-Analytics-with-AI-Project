# IBM SkillsBuild Data Analytics with AI Academic Internship Project

**Project Title:** Retail Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Report  
**Student Name:** Avinash Kumar Singh

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
The goal of this project is to implement a Data Analytics framework to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights. It utilizes a 5-level analytical hierarchy: KPIs, Trends, Drivers, Risks/Opportunities, and Recommendations.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** Total sales generated across categories.
- **Total Orders:** Count of distinct customer orders.
- **Churn Risk:** Identification of active customers vs churn-prone customers.
